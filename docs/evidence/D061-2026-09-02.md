Status: historical implementation evidence only. This record captures the 14-package phase-A snapshot before tensor-reverse and SafeTensors integration. The current manifests contain 16 packages, 18 test suites, and 11 benchmarks. D-061 through D-076 remain `Proposed`.

## Package and API topology

- This phase-A snapshot contained 14 packages, 15 test suites, and 11 benchmarks.
- The root library depends only on `base`.
- Numerical, sampling, learning, dense exact, benchmark, and private legacy IR sources are in their owning packages.
- `Markovian.Action` owns `ActionId` and gives its type parameter a nominal role.
- `ExactFiniteDist` has no `Applicative` or `Monad` instance. Its safe `Functor`, `Foldable`, and `Traversable` instances preserve support and masses.
- The distribution representation is opaque, and no unchecked bind helper is exported.
- Exact bind and exact kernel composition require explicit limits and return typed failures.
- Focused fixtures cover exact and one-below result, work, numerator-bit, and denominator-bit limits; 64-by-64 admission; early 65-by-65 rejection at entry 4097; infinite input rejection; duplicate preservation; atomic continuation failure; deterministic reports; and admitted identity and associativity.
