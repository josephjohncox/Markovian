Reviewed PR head `fdfc1f868c0c0773c35c4cae9ec260bcc01ab4cd` against
`80949faed2da16d603d7eebd0586ea9d94b325f9` on 2026-09-11. The review covered

## Defects corrected

- CI stopped at `scripts/check-learning --run` because the committed teaching
  receipt did not match the implementation sources. The original failure is in
  [job 103488648460](https://github.com/josephjohncox/Markovian/actions/runs/34669713890/job/103488648460).
  The receipt was regenerated by compiling and executing the teaching fixtures;
  the recorded outputs did not change.
- `scripts/check-correlated-solver` was absent from CI and `Markovian.cabal`'s
  source manifest. CI now runs it from the checkout and unpacked root archive.
  The script exercises private fixtures at both `-O0` and `-O2`; CI also runs
  the public root suite at `-O2`.
- The private solver fixture lacked `ImportQualifiedPost`, which the standalone
  formatter could not infer from a Cabal component. The solver files also failed
  formatting and HLint checks. The fixture now declares its extension, ordinary
  lint suggestions are fixed, and narrow annotations retain the contract's
  explicit traversal order and the oracle's coordinate notation.
- The solver's Rational observer used the larger of the configured limit and
  machine ceiling, so an oversized input could be scanned long after its first
  failing bit. It now uses the smaller bound and shares that bound between the
  numerator and denominator. The regression checks exact and capped combined
  sizes, including signed values and denominator exhaustion. It failed against
  the previous scanner on `3/256` with a two-bit limit.
- Current status documents still called D-083 unimplemented. They now identify
  its implementation and outstanding review. The capability inventory includes
  D-079, D-080, D-081 and D-083 with their actual availability and decision status.
  The capability tests no longer require the old D-083 implementation-pending
  wording or a fixed D-081 sentence in every document.
- The README, installation guide and solver comments remove repeated process
  narration. The PR description summarizes behavior, validation and remaining
  limits. Frozen contracts and historical evidence files retain their contents.

## Remaining D-083 work

The verification contract remains incomplete. In particular:

- Section 11.8 requires test instrumentation of the constructor/checker's
  ordered Rational intermediates and comparison with each shadow sequence.
  `testCheckerRowSequenceAgreement` compares returned row results. It does not
  capture discarded intermediates. `correlationSolveCheckerCoveredRationalBits`
  is calculated from the shadow, without a measurement of the actual checker.
- The public/private tests do not complete the section 11.5 redundant-row
  controls or every section 11.8 competing-failure case, including direct
  observations of successor-reservation exhaustion and candidate rejection
  before elimination. Source-loop/reservation and strictness evidence
  also remains required by the frozen contract.

These are acceptance gaps. D-083 stays Proposed. The review does not change
released package versions or certify CUDA hardware behavior.
